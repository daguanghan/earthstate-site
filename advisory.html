Advisory

Engineering advice, backed by our own tools

We work with infrastructure owners, engineering consultancies and research partners. Because we build the platforms ourselves, we can show exactly how a result was reached, and change the method when your asset needs it.

01

Capacity and remaining-life assessment

For lattice towers, steel structures and bridges. We build a checkable model from drawings, inspection records or point clouds, check it against the Eurocodes, and return utilisation, remaining margin and a ranked repair list.

Typical outputs: assessment report, model files, evidence pack, repair ranking.

02

Storm and climate resilience

Line-level wind studies for network owners: which towers come closest to failure in which storms, and which strengthening options buy the most margin for the money.

Typical outputs: line screening, collapse-sweep results, strengthening comparison.

03

Infrastructure AI advisory and assurance

What AI should and should not decide on a live asset, how to verify it, and how to keep an audit trail an engineer and a regulator can follow.

Typical outputs: assurance review, verification plan, governance and sign-off design.

04

Digital delivery and information management

ISO 19650 information requirements, IFC models, COBie handover and data architecture, so that asset data can feed analysis rather than sit in a drawer.

Typical outputs: OIR, AIR and EIR, information model, data architecture.

Experience behind the advice

Advice from someone who has done the work

The founder's advisory experience comes from real assets and real programmes: capacity assessment and repair ranking of in-service transmission towers for a network operator, laser scanning and assessment of existing bridges, a data architecture for a steel bridge programme, building a research institute and its industry partnerships, and international research collaboration across Europe and Asia.

Projects completed in previous academic posts are described on the Work page by asset and decision, not by client.

Expert advisory network

The right specialist for each question

EarthState works with an expert advisory network of academics and senior engineers in the UK and Europe, covering structural and wind engineering, power-system engineering, sensing and monitoring, BIM and information management, and software and AI development.

Experts are brought in per project, named in the scope, and work under the same quality and data-handling rules as the practice.
