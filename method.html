Method

A result you can trace back to its source

Ageing infrastructure decisions are only as good as the chain of evidence behind them. Our method keeps that chain intact, from the first drawing to the engineer's signature.

01Drawings and dataDrawings, point clouds, inspection records, sensor and weather data, each logged with its source.
02Checkable modelA member-level model built from the drawings, with assumptions written down.
03Code checkLoads and resistance to the Eurocodes and UK National Annexes.
04Capacity statementUtilisation, remaining margin, and what to repair first.
05Engineer sign-offA report and evidence pack that a reviewer can trace end to end.
PRINCIPLE 01

Numbers come from calculations

Language models help people operate the tools. Every value in a result comes from a calculation step that can be named, rerun and checked.

PRINCIPLE 02

The engineer stays in the loop

The platforms prepare evidence. They do not approve their own work; a qualified engineer reviews and signs.

PRINCIPLE 03

Everything is labelled

Measured, derived, provisional and synthetic data are marked as such on every screen, in every report.

PRINCIPLE 04

Evidence is sealed

Reports ship with an evidence pack and a SHA-256 manifest, so an assessment can be handed over and verified later.

PRINCIPLE 05

Data stay where they should

Partner data are processed in the United Kingdom, used only for the project, and can run on a local model that never leaves the machine.

PRINCIPLE 06

Standards, named

Every check states the standard and clause it rests on, so a reviewer knows what was assumed.
