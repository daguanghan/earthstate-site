Collaborate

Research prototypes, open to joint work

EarthState is a series of research prototypes developed by Dr Daguang Han's research team. The team works with universities, infrastructure owners and engineering companies who want to try the methods on a real question. Because it builds the prototypes itself, it can show exactly how a result was reached, and change the method when an asset needs it.

Questions the prototypes address

Four questions about ageing infrastructure

01

Capacity and remaining life

For lattice towers, steel structures and bridges. A checkable model is built from drawings, inspection records or point clouds and checked against the Eurocodes, giving utilisation, remaining margin and a ranked repair list.

What a case study produces: assessment report, model files, evidence pack, repair ranking.

02

Storm and climate resilience

Line-level wind studies: which towers come closest to failure in which storms, and which strengthening options buy the most margin.

What a case study produces: line screening, collapse-sweep results, strengthening comparison.

03

Assurance of AI on infrastructure

What AI should and should not decide on a live asset, how to verify it, and how to keep an audit trail an engineer and a regulator can follow.

What a case study produces: assurance review, verification plan, governance and sign-off design.

04

Digital delivery and information management

ISO 19650 information requirements, IFC models, COBie handover and data architecture, so that asset data can feed analysis rather than sit in a drawer.

What a case study produces: OIR, AIR and EIR, information model, data architecture.

Ways to work together

From a first look to a joint project

01

Open demos

Try the browser demos on demonstration data. No sign-in is needed.

02

Walkthrough or seminar

A live session on demonstration data for an engineering team or a research group, online or in person.

03

Joint research and innovation project

A proposal or a study with a university or a company. The prototypes can be run on a partner's asset inside the project, with papers and intellectual property agreed at the start.

Joint work is scoped around the question to be answered. Partner data are processed in the United Kingdom and are not shared outside the project.

Experience behind the prototypes

Built by people who have done the work

Dr Han's experience comes from real assets and real programmes: capacity assessment and repair ranking of in-service transmission towers for a network operator, laser scanning and assessment of existing bridges, a data architecture for a steel bridge programme, building a research institute and its industry partnerships, and international research collaboration across Europe and Asia.

Projects from his academic and institute roles are described on the Work page by asset and decision; owners are not named.

Expert network

The right specialist for each question

The team works with a network of academics and senior engineers in the UK and Europe, covering structural and wind engineering, power-system engineering, sensing and monitoring, BIM and information management, and software and AI development.

Specialists join by project and are named in it.
