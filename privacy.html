Who we are

This website is run by Dr Daguang Han, Loughborough, United Kingdom. EarthState is the name of a series of research prototypes developed by his research team. Contact: daguang.han@gmail.com.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the 3D viewer loads open-source libraries from a public content delivery network; those services receive your IP address as part of delivering the files.

When you email us

If you email us, we use your name, address and message only to reply and to follow up on what you ask about. We keep correspondence for as long as that needs, or as the law requires, and do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Write to the address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
