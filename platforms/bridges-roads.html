Product 03 · EarthState Bridges & Roads

When the drawings no longer match the bridge

EarthState Bridges & Roads starts from what is really there. A scan or a drawing set becomes a model that can be assessed, and repairs are ranked by the capacity that remains.

In development

Bridges

From scan to an assessable model

The bridge product follows the same line as EarthState Grid, with the bridge's own components, failure modes and assessment standards.

  • Drawings or a laser scan to a member-level model
  • Capacity checked to the assessment standards
  • Repairs ranked by remaining capacity
  • Failure modes and a maintenance programme, then the report
91%Intersection over Union against critical structural outlines, on a real bridge

Published results from the founder's peer-reviewed work on point-cloud modelling of real and experimental bridges. They describe the method, not this product, which is still being built. Sources: Proceedings of the Institution of Civil Engineers – Smart Infrastructure and Construction, 2025 (doi:10.1680/jsmic.24.00012) for the first two figures; Advances in Civil Engineering, 2021 (doi:10.1155/2021/9916859), on a reduced-scale experimental arch bridge, for the third.

Roads

Roads follow on the same core

Road condition from scans is on the roadmap. It will use the same modelling, risk ranking and audit trail as the bridge product. Nothing is shown for roads yet.

Data

Open or synthetic data only

Demonstration cases will use open datasets or synthetic structures, with the source and the limits written on the page. No client structure is shown.
