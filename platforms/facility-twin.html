Product 04 · EarthState Facilities

A building in operation, as a twin the team can use every day

In development

FacilityNexus, the working prototype behind EarthState Facilities, places a facility on a 3D twin base and connects what happens in it, from events and equipment health to the know-how of the people who run it, with an AI assistant on every page.

01

3D twin base

Buildings, floors, rooms and assets in one navigable model, linked to the asset register.

02

Event centre

Events shown on the model where they happen, with their history.

03

Equipment health

Diagnostics for plant and equipment, so maintenance goes where it is needed first.

04

Knowledge base

Operators build and update the facility's knowledge base, so procedures stay with the building.

05

Wearable AI badges

Badges that connect staff working on site to the assistant.

06

AI assistant on every page

Ask about any asset, event or procedure in plain language, in English, German or Chinese.

Airports

An airport pack In development

The first sector case for EarthState Facilities is an airport: terminal and airfield equipment, from baggage systems and boarding bridges to lifts, air handling and airfield lighting, ranked by its effect on punctuality, passenger safety and disruption.

  • One criticality ranking across terminal and airfield assets
  • Failure modes to maintenance tasks for a baggage line or a boarding bridge
  • Weather, flight schedule, passenger peaks and equipment condition together, 72 hours ahead

The airport case will run on a synthetic airport. Nothing is shown for it yet.
